Location Onboarding Kit

Get every location verified, without the back-and-forth.

Onboarding a 100-branch brand stalls in two places: chasing the client for clean branch data, and Google rejecting or delaying verification. This kit catches the problems before upload, turns them into one clear request for the client, and tracks every location until it is verified.

Everything runs in your browser. Files you upload are never sent anywhere.

STEP 1CollectSend the branch template and a request that names who owns each piece of data.
STEP 2CheckUpload what comes back. Every location is checked against Google's rules.
STEP 3FixSend the client one fix list, then export a clean bulk upload file.
STEP 4VerifyTrack each location to verified, and export the tracker to Zoho.

Step 1 · Collect

Ask once, ask the right people

Large clients spread branch data across facilities, IT, brand and regional teams, so a single request to marketing waits weeks. Name an owner for each piece of data from day one.

Client details

The template follows Google's bulk upload columns, plus three onboarding columns: location type, signage and an on-site contact for video verification.
